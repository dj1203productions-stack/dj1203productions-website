Before you book

Service agreement summary

This is a summary of the DJ1203 Productions, LLC service agreement. A full contract is provided after booking and must be signed to confirm your event. Tap a section to open it.

📌 Client responsibilities
  • Provide all final event details at least 30 days before the event.
  • Maintain a safe, secure and suitable venue.
  • Minimum setup space: 8 ft × 6 ft.
  • Guests and vendors may not touch DJ equipment.
  • Let us know about any venue restrictions.
  • Start/end time changes must be requested in writing at least 14 days before the event. Later changes depend on availability and may incur fees.
  • If the event starts late for reasons outside our control, the end time stays the same unless overtime is approved.
🎧 Services provided
  • Professional DJ & MC services, sound, lighting and any selected add-ons. Setup and teardown are included.
💰 Payments & retainer
  • A 50% non-refundable retainer is due at signing to book your date.
  • The remaining balance is due 14 days before the event.
  • Late payments incur a $25/day fee.
  • Accepted payment methods: PayPal and credit/debit card.
⏰ Extra time & overtime
  • Extra hours: $250/hr until midnight, $450/hr after midnight, billed in 30-minute increments.
  • Overtime must be approved by the Client or the named Day-of Contact before the scheduled end time. Requests from guests or other vendors are not accepted without that approval.
  • Overtime is due the night of the event and depends on venue rules and DJ availability.
🚗 Travel & mileage
  • 0–25 miles: included
  • 26–50 miles: +$150
  • 51–75 miles: +$300
  • 76–100 miles: +$450
  • 100+ miles: +$600
  • Overnight/long-distance events: client provides hotel (3-star minimum), parking and transportation; travel day fee $300/day; DJ meals up to $75/day; equipment transport fee $150 if flying.
⚡ Setup, power & venue
  • Typical setup: 90 minutes (standard events); 2 hours (weddings and full-production events). Teardown: 60 minutes.
  • The setup area must be cleared, clean and on a flat, stable surface before we arrive. Delays over 15 minutes may reduce performance time.
  • Power: two dedicated 15-amp circuits within 25 feet of the DJ area, not shared with other vendors.
  • Outdoor events: full overhead shelter, sidewalls if needed, dry level ground and a backup indoor plan (or add our Outdoor Tent Rental, $150).
  • Load-in fees: stairs without elevator $50/flight; carries of 100–250 ft $75, 250–500 ft $150, 500+ ft $250. Please disclose stairs and carry distance at booking.
🌦️ Weather & emergencies
  • We may pause, stop or move equipment if rain, high wind, lightning, extreme heat/cold or power loss puts people or equipment at risk.
  • If the event is shortened or stopped for reasons outside our control, the full contracted fee is still due.
  • If you move the event to a new date for these reasons, we’ll try to accommodate it, subject to availability.
☕ Breaks, food & beverage
  • The DJ may take brief restroom/hydration breaks; music continues at all times.
  • For events of 4+ hours, longer breaks may occur during natural pauses.
  • Client provides water and non-alcoholic beverages.
  • For events of 5+ hours, client provides a meal or allows the DJ to bring food.
  • The DJ will not consume alcohol.
🎵 Music requests
  • Requests must be submitted 14 days before the event.
  • The DJ may refuse explicit or unsafe music.
🚫 Cancellation & rescheduling
  • The retainer is non-refundable.
  • Cancellations within 30 days require full payment of the balance.
  • Rescheduling must be in writing and is subject to availability.
  • Rescheduling within 30 days: $100 fee.
  • If the DJ cancels, all payments are refunded.
🛡️ Liability & insurance
  • Client holds DJ1203 Productions harmless for claims arising from guests, venue issues, weather or circumstances beyond our control.
  • DJ1203 Productions carries general liability insurance and will provide a Certificate of Insurance on request. Please request it at least 14 days before the event.
🔒 Safety, security & conduct
  • Client must maintain adequate security before, during and after the event.
  • Client is responsible for theft, vandalism or damage caused by guests, attendees or venue staff, including dropped microphones.
  • If threats, violence or unsafe conditions occur, the DJ may stop immediately and will allow up to 30 minutes for resolution before deciding whether to resume.
  • Client remains responsible for full payment and any damages.
👔 DJ dress code
  • Weddings & formal: dress pants, dress shoes, button-down shirt; optional tie/blazer.
  • Corporate: business-casual or business-professional.
  • School / youth / casual: clean, professional casual; branded apparel allowed.
  • Outdoor: weather-appropriate clothing.
🎖️ Hometown Heroes discount
  • 10% off any base package for active or retired military, police, fire, EMS, nurses, teachers and truck drivers. Proof may be requested.
📸 Media release
  • Clients choose whether DJ1203 Productions may use photos or video clips from the event for promotional purposes.
🖊️ Full contract & signatures
  • The full contract will be provided after booking. It must be signed along with the retainer to officially secure your event date.
  • If the client is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must also sign and accepts responsibility for payment and all obligations.
