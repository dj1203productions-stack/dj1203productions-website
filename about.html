About DJ1203

South Bend’s hometown DJ

Built on hard work and honoring the people who keep our community running.

Our mission

From weddings to school dances, our mission is to give every client pro-level sound, lighting and energy, and to treat them like neighbors, not invoices.

We read the room, play the right song at the right moment, and bring a lighting rig that makes the night feel bigger. You get a DJ who shows up early, communicates clearly and gives every event 110%.

  • Weddings, quinceañeras, school and church dances, college, corporate and private parties
  • Clean, radio-friendly sets for school and youth events
  • DJ1203 Productions, LLC: fully insured, with COIs for your venue

Hometown Heroes

Thank you for what you do

We proudly offer 10% off any base package to the people who keep our communities running:

  • Active-duty and retired military, Guard and Reserve
  • First responders: police, fire and EMS
  • Nurses
  • Teachers
  • Truck drivers

Just show a valid ID or proof of service when you book, such as a Military ID, DD‑214, department or employer ID, school ID or CDL. Applies to personal events.
