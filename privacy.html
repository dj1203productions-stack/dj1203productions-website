Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

DJ1203 Productions, LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you fill out our booking request form, we collect your name, email, phone number, event details and any files you choose to attach. This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, and our booking form and store are provided by EMP DJ and Ecwid, which may collect basic visit data such as your IP address and browser type.

How we use it

We use your information only to answer your inquiry, plan and book your event, send quotes, invoices and agreements, and stay in touch about your event.

Payments

Payments are handled by secure third-party processors such as PayPal. We never see or store your full card or bank details.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We only share what’s needed with trusted partners helping with your event (for example, a backup DJ or your venue), or when required by law.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may text you about your event. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for your event and our business records.

Questions

Contact us at dj1203productions@gmail.com with any questions or to ask that we update or delete your information.
